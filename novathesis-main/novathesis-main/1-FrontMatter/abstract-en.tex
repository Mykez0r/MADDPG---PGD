Multi-agent deep deterministic policy gradient (MADDPG) with Graphical Neural Networks (GNN) is a powerful coordination learning system for Deep Reinforcement Learning (DRL) agents that has the potential to solve critical decision-making problems in packet routing while improving overall network performance. In recent studies, the importance of integrating DRL, MADDPG, and GNN algorithms in network routing systems for addressing current problems is evident. However, the security implications and study of vulnerabilities and consequences in this area are still relatively unknown. It is of interest to study how these vulnerabilities can be exploited and their consequences understood so that adequate defenses can be developed.

Known attack algorithms are implemented, adapted for the network context, against the most common architectures of MADDPG and GNN separately and then a joint GNN-MADDPG implementation to see the effect on their performance.
